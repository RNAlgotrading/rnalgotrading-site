RN TradeLab

Release Notes

What's changed in each version. RN TradeLab updates itself with a friendly in-app notice - see how updating works.

v0.7 Current

Released August 2026
  • New: Instant parameter tuning. Right-click any chart for "Edit Indicators" or "Edit Strategy" - try a different EMA length or SuperTrend multiplier on the spot, no full optimization run needed. Changes apply immediately for the rest of your session.
  • New: Prev Session Levels. A ready-made indicator showing yesterday's typical price, high and low, plus today's own open - four reference lines that reset each session, genuinely useful for intraday work.
  • New: Session markers. A subtle line now marks the end of every trading session on the chart, so consecutive days are easy to tell apart at a glance.
  • Improved: the Chart tab now opens showing the most recent price action, not the oldest candles in your history.

v0.6

Released August 2026
  • RN TradeLab is now free. Full access, no card, no price anywhere in the app. Just sign up and start using it.
  • New: Live Chart. Watch the current candle update every second, straight from your broker, while the market is open - see your strategy react to price in real time, not just in backtests.
  • New: Fundamental Analysis. Type any symbol for a free snapshot - valuation ratios, a revenue/profit trend chart, and shareholding pattern, right inside the Knowledge Base tab.
  • New: Write your own indicators. Studies now has Indicators alongside Strategies - write and save your own the same way you already build strategies, then apply them straight to a live chart. MACD, RSI, SuperTrend and more are built in, each showing on its own clean panel.
  • Charts that actually fit your screen. Resize the window or stack on more indicators - the chart reshapes with you, never shrinks or clips.
  • Smarter currency display. Backtest and Portfolio now correctly show Rs or $ depending on the data you're looking at.

v0.5

Released August 2026
  • Import your own CSV data. A new CSV Files tab lets you bring your own data - your columns are auto-detected, and if we're not confident, you get a quick confirm screen with a live preview before anything is imported. A panel always shows what was read from your file next to the expected format.
  • New Chart tab. Pick a symbol, timeframe and strategy and watch the candlestick chart auto-refresh live - a fast, single-symbol playground before committing to a full multi-symbol backtest.
  • Data Center. The old Download tab is now organised into Broker, Yahoo Finance and CSV Files - three clean, focused screens instead of one crowded page.
  • Configurable Train/Test split. Drag it from the classic 70/30 all the way to 100/0 and watch in-sample-only results collapse the moment real out-of-sample data is judged - a vivid, hands-on look at overfitting.
  • Abort a running backtest and see whatever completed, instead of waiting it out or restarting the app.
  • Fixed: a startup crash affecting some users with previously CSV-imported data.

v0.4

Released August 2026
  • New SuperTrend indicator and strategy. Drawn in two colours on the chart - green while it supports price, red while it resists it - and available as a ready-made "SuperTrend Flip" strategy whose stop trails the indicator's own line.
  • Slippage and Commission. Optional, realistic trading-cost assumptions you can set once and apply to every backtest and optimization - see how a strategy holds up after real-world friction, not just on paper.
  • One Settings window. Capital, trading costs, entry filters and risk limits now live in a single Settings dialog instead of scattered buttons.
  • Flexible exits. Choose Stoploss, Profit Target, Trailing Stop and Time-based Exit independently - check as many as you like, and whichever a trade reaches first is the one that closes it. Applied consistently across every strategy.
  • Chart improvements: a Volume panel, hover over any candle to see its OHLC/volume and every active indicator's value, and click a trade in the trade list to jump the chart straight to it.

v0.3

Released July 2026
  • Volume on the trade chart. A new Volume panel sits beneath the candles, colour-matched to each candle's direction.
  • Add any indicator to the chart, no code needed. A new "+ Add indicator" picker lets you drop EMA, SMA, Bollinger Bands or RSI onto the chart with your own period, to explore how it would have looked - separate from whatever the strategy itself trades on. RSI gets its own panel automatically.
  • Hover for details. Hover over any candle to see its open/high/low/close, volume, and the value of every active indicator at that exact point.
  • Jump straight to a trade. Click a row in the trade list and the chart jumps right to it, instead of scrolling to find it.
  • Fixed: a crash that could occur when opening the trade chart from a fresh click.

v0.2

Released July 2026
  • Filter your live market view. Top Gainers/Losers, Sector Heatmap and Gap & Momentum Movers now let you narrow to Nifty 50, 100, 200, or any custom dataset you've built - instead of always seeing your whole universe.
  • See your strategy's indicators on the chart. When you open a strategy's trades on the candlestick chart, its indicators (EMA, Bollinger Bands, VWAP) now appear automatically - no setup needed.
  • Account improvements. Proper email validation, a password reset option if you ever get locked out, and stronger protection around your account.

v0.1

Released July 2026
  • Optimize tab. Tune any strategy's inputs with Start/Stop/Increment ranges. Results rank on in-sample data with the out-of-sample column beside them, so overfitting has nowhere to hide.
  • 15 example strategies - MACD, RSI, Bollinger (both breakout and mean-reversion), Stochastic, breakouts, gaps, VWAP and more - commented as learning references and tunable in the Optimize tab.
  • Candlestick trade chart. See every trade drawn on the candles: entry and exit joined by a dotted line, stoploss and target levels, and a tinted background showing exactly where out-of-sample data begins.
  • Free NSE data via Yahoo Finance - no broker account needed to start.
  • Knowledge Base: Top Gainers/Losers, Gap & Momentum Movers, and a searchable Trading Glossary.
  • In-app update notifications so you always know when a new version is available.
